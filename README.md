# Crop Doctor: AI se fasal ki bimari ki pehchan

## Summary
Ye project kisanon ke liye ek AI app ka idea hai jo patte ki photo dekh kar fasal ki bimari pehchanta hai aur ilaaj ka asan mashwara deta hai.

Building AI course project

## Background
Choti kheti karne walon ko aksar fasal ki bimari ka late pata chalta hai, aur expert tak pahunchna mushkil hota hai. Is se fasal kharab hoti hai aur nuksan hota hai. Meri motivation ye hai ke kisan ko foran aur sasta mashwara mile.

## Data and AI techniques
Data: patton ki labelled photos (jaise open dataset PlantVillage). Technique: image classification, yani convolutional neural network (CNN) jo photo se bimari ka naam batata hai.

## How is it used?
Kisan phone se patte ki photo kheenchta hai, app bimari ka naam aur ilaaj ke basic steps dikhata hai.

## Challenges
Har ilaqe aur mausam ki photos alag hoti hain, isliye model kabhi galat andaza laga sakta hai. Ye expert ki jagah nahi le sakta.

## What next?
Local zubaan (Urdu) support, offline mode, aur ilaqe ke hisaab se dawai ki salah shamil ki ja sakti hai.
